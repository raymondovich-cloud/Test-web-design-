## What is fixed at this point

### Product architecture

- Finance, Health and Development are treated as independent domains.
- Each domain is intended to calculate its own independent index.
- A future cross-domain layer is approved conceptually:
  `Finance Index + Health Index + Development Index → Life System Interaction Engine → Life System State → Life Quality Index`.
- The Interaction Engine must not create direct domain-to-domain dependencies.
- The three component indexes must not be rewritten by the Interaction Engine.
- Life Quality Index must not be implemented as a simple arithmetic average without a separately approved model.

### Finance

- Finance is the first established calculation domain.
- FSI 2.1 is the current finance methodology baseline.
- Finance contains the established financial data blocks and analytics model.
- Assets/liquidity handling and the current Finance UI are part of this baseline.

### Health

- Health is in active product/UI development.
- Health data sections have numbering aligned with Finance.
- Health data sections have tooltips aligned with Finance.
- The collapsed Health edit state does not use the unnecessary expansion arrow.
- Apple Health integration is an architectural direction, not yet a completed integration.

### Development

- Development Health 1.0 methodology is approved and stored in:
  `docs/development-1.0-methodology.md`
- The methodology defines five factors:
  - Direction — 20%
  - Goals — 30%
  - Growth — 25%
  - Execution — 15%
  - Balance — 10%
- The methodology defines a 0–100 Development Health index, coverage, confidence, recency and constraint concepts.
- Calculation code for Development Health has **not** yet been implemented.

### Life Quality

- Life Quality Index is a future system-level result.
- Its final calculation model is intentionally not frozen yet.
- The approved architectural direction is to place the Interaction Engine between the three independent domain indexes and Life Quality.

## Explicitly not frozen yet

The following remain open for the next stage:

1. Interaction Engine contract and input/output schema.
2. Cross-domain interaction rules.
3. Life System State model.
4. Life Quality Index calculation model.
5. Development Health calculation parameters after scenario validation.
6. Apple Health production integration.
7. Further product/UI implementation beyond the current baseline.
